# Artwork attribution

The original project artwork in `assets/concepts/`, `assets/meadows/` and
`status-site/public/art/` is offered under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](LICENSE), to the extent the
project holds applicable rights. Credit **Valheim Impact contributors**, link
to [the project](https://github.com/Sikbik/valheim-impact), include the license
link, and indicate your modifications. See the per-file provenance registries
for exact source identities and transformations.

Future contributions must provide the contributor's preferred public credit
and any required third-party attribution. A pseudonym is welcome. Do not submit
personal contact information in artwork metadata.

Valheim's name, game, source asset names and metadata belong to their respective
rights holders. The project license does not grant rights to game assets,
trademarks, third-party packages or other material the project does not own.
No original game texture pixels or mesh geometry are included here.

Concept illustrations describe an art direction. They are not game screenshots
or proof that a biome has been completed.
